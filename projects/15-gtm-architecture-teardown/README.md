# Project 15: Public GTM Architecture Teardown

> **Status:** 📋 Not published. An earlier draft was removed on review: it contained out-of-date or wrong prices and claims with no source.

## Problem

GTM engineers are expected to explain how a company's go-to-market works: pricing, the path from free to paid, and the signals that trigger sales.

## Proposed solution

A written teardown of two or three companies, from public pages only.

## Rules before it is published

- Every price has a link to the company's own pricing page and the date it was read.
- Every statement about how a company works internally has a public source, or is labelled as the author's inference.
- No invented metrics or motives.
