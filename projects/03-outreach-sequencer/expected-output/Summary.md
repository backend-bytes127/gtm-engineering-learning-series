# Sequence results

## What worked

- **Simulated results.** Nothing was sent. Outcomes come from a seeded random model with made-up rates, so use this report to see its shape, not to plan a campaign.
- 25 contacts entered the sequence and 86 messages were sent (57 email, 29 LinkedIn).
- 1 contact(s) were held back before launch because a message could not be personalised.
- 4 of 25 contacts replied (16%) and 1 booked a meeting.
- Channel comparison: only 4 replies in all (3 email, 1 LinkedIn); a gap that small is noise, so no channel is called better.
- LinkedIn invites (step 2): 10 of 20 accepted (50%).
- Most first replies came from step 1 (email, template `intro_email`): 2 of 4.
- List hygiene: 1 of 22 emailed contacts bounced; verify addresses with an SMTP-level checker before the next send.
- 1 contact(s) unsubscribed and are now on the suppression list.
